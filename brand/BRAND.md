# LeoLend brand

Source of truth: [leolend.com](https://leolend.com). Values here were taken from the live site.

## Name
- Brand: **LeoLend** (one word, capital L twice). Legal line: **LeoLENDINGroup**. Monogram: **LLG**.
- Positioning: premium merchant cash advance and revenue-based financing for high-growth businesses seeking rapid capital.

## Colour
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--ll-bg` | `#0d1f17` | Page background (forest) |
| `--ll-bg-deep` | `#0a1d14` | Footer, wells |
| `--ll-card` | `#1a3a28` | Cards, panels |
| `--ll-gold` | `#c9a840` | Primary: buttons, links, accents |
| `--ll-gold-bright` | `#e0b94b` | Hover, highlights |
| `--ll-gold-deep` | `#8a7440` | Gold on light surfaces |
| `--ll-text` | `#e8e8e8` | Body text on dark |
| `--ll-text-muted` | `#8a9a8e` | Secondary text |
| `--ll-cream` / `--ll-ink` | `#e6e1d8` / `#1a1a1a` | Light surfaces (email, print) |
| `--ll-success` | `#8fd6a8` | Positive status |

Forest and gold only. Gold is for action and emphasis; never use it for long body text.

## Type
- Headlines: **Archivo Black**, uppercase-friendly, tight tracking.
- Body: **Inter** 400–700.
- Labels / numbers: **Space Grotesk** 500–700, wide tracking for eyebrow labels.

## Logo
- `brand/logo-round.png` — LLG lion roundel, transparent background. Default on every surface. `brand/logo.png` is the original on a white square. Keep clear space of at least a quarter of its width; never recolour or stretch.
- `brand/icon.png` — small mark for tight spaces.

## Required legal line
Any merchant-facing surface (email, landing page, SMS landing, PDF) carries:

> Merchant Cash Advances are purchases of future receivables and are not loans. Terms, amounts, and availability vary by business qualifications. Agreements and policies are subject to change.

Never call an MCA a "loan", quote an "interest rate" or "APR" for it, or promise approval.
